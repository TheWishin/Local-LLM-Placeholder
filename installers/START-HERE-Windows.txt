========================================================
  Data Anonymizer  -  START HERE (Windows)
========================================================

The easy way (recommended)
--------------------------
1. Double-click:  Install-Windows.bat
2. The first time, it installs the local AI (Ollama) and downloads a
   small model automatically. This can take a few minutes - just wait.
3. Your browser opens at http://localhost:5100. That's the app.
4. To stop: close the black window.

Next time, just double-click Install-Windows.bat again - it starts
instantly because everything is already installed.

Only want to run the app (skip the AI)?
---------------------------------------
Double-click  DataAnonymizer.exe  directly. Pattern detection still
works; the optional AI (names without a salutation, health data, etc.)
just won't be available until Ollama is installed.

Is it private?
--------------
Yes. Everything runs on this computer. No text is ever sent to the
internet. The optional AI (Ollama) also runs fully locally.

Windows SmartScreen warning?
----------------------------
The app is not code-signed, so Windows may show "Windows protected your
PC". Click "More info" -> "Run anyway". It is safe - it is just an
unsigned local program.
